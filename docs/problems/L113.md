# L113: rotate=90 on columns turns their picture: orientation=vertical lays them out along the roll

- Severity: note (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: layout

## What to do

Nothing to fix if you want the picture turned. For a label read sideways, write orientation=vertical on the columns instead of rotate=: they are laid out along the roll, length= long (72 mm by default).

In the terminal: `bobina help L113`. All layout codes and their meaning: [layout](layout.md); every category: [index](README.md).
