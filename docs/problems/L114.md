# L114: turned columns: a block cut across its lines between two labels (read them together)

- Severity: warning (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: layout

## What to do

Nothing is lost: the block goes on in the next label, cut across a line. Read the labels together, or make it fit: a longer length=, a smaller size= or width=, less in it, or take it out of the turned columns.

In the terminal: `bobina help L114`. All layout codes and their meaning: [layout](layout.md); every category: [index](README.md).
