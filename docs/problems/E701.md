# E701: could not check for a new bobina (no network, or PyPI did not answer)

- Severity: error (stops the job: nothing prints)
- Category: errors

## What to do

Try again when online; the releases are also listed at https://github.com/rafaelbressan/bobina/releases.

In the terminal: `bobina help E701`. All codes: [index](README.md).
