# L112: turned columns: too tall (more labels, or a block drawn smaller), or meant by rotate=90

- Severity: note (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: layout

## What to do

Turned columns are as tall as the paper is wide. Too tall: give the label more length (length=, or length=auto), use less text or smaller sizes, or wrap=wrap to move a column to a label of its own; a block drawn smaller: give it a smaller width= or size=. rotate=90 on columns turns their picture: for a label read sideways write orientation=vertical instead (72 mm long unless length= says).

In the terminal: `bobina help L112`. All layout codes and their meaning: [layout](layout.md); every category: [index](README.md).
