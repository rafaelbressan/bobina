# L112: turned columns too tall for the label: they go on in more labels, or a block drawn smaller

- Severity: note (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: layout

## What to do

Turned columns are as tall as the paper is wide. Give the label more length (length=, or length=auto), use less text or smaller sizes, or wrap=wrap to move a column to a label of its own. A block drawn smaller or cut between two labels: give it a smaller width= or size=, or take it out of the turned columns.

In the terminal: `bobina help L112`. All layout codes and their meaning: [layout](layout.md); every category: [index](README.md).
