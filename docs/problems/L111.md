# L111: no room for a box or columns here: what they hold printed without them

- Severity: warning (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: layout

## What to do

Put fewer boxes, quotes or lists around it: each takes letters from the line, and a box or columns need some room. Without it, what they hold prints plainly, one block under the other.

In the terminal: `bobina help L111`. All layout codes and their meaning: [layout](layout.md); every category: [index](README.md).
