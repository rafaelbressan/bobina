# F206: small white-on-black text printed bold: ink fills thin white strokes

- Severity: note (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: fonts

## What to do

Nothing to fix: white text on black below raster.invert_bold_below_px (48 by default) prints bold, or ink fills its thin strokes. To keep its weight, make it bigger (size=2x, 48px); a printer whose paper holds thin white text: 0 in its profile (docs/guides/white-text.md).

In the terminal: `bobina help F206`. All fonts codes and their meaning: [fonts](fonts.md); every category: [index](README.md).
