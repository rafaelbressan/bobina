# F204: inverted raster text too thin or small

- Severity: warning (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: fonts

## What to do

Inverted raster text needs at least 20 px to stay readable (below 48 px bobina makes it bold).

Evidence: printed test R2-12 (docs/printers/mtp-ii-58.md).

In the terminal: `bobina help F204`. All fonts codes and their meaning: [fonts](fonts.md); every category: [index](README.md).
