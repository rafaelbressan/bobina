# E402: printer unreachable

- Severity: error (stops the job: nothing prints)
- Category: errors

## What to do

Is the printer on and in range? Through a command (ssh): is that machine on? The message names the printer and what the command said. Look at the paper before printing again: part of the job may be there.

In the terminal: `bobina help E402`. All codes: [index](README.md).
