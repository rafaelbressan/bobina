# Q115: a code in an inverted box without its whole white margin, or white on black

- Severity: warning (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: codes

## What to do

Give the code room for its margin (a smaller module= or width=), keep it standing (no turned columns, a right angle), or put it after the inverted box's closing :::.

In the terminal: `bobina help Q115`. All codes codes and their meaning: [codes](codes.md); every category: [index](README.md).
