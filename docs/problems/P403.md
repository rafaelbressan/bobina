# P403: a USB printer this user cannot write to

- Severity: warning (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: printer and paper

## What to do

Add the user to the lp group (sudo usermod -aG lp $USER), then log out and in again.

In the terminal: `bobina help P403`. All printer and paper codes and their meaning: [printer and paper](printer-and-paper.md); every category: [index](README.md).
