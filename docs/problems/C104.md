# C104: chart dates out of order: drawn in the order written, evenly spaced

- Severity: note (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: charts

## What to do

Sort the rows by date (oldest first) and the points are spaced by the time between them.

In the terminal: `bobina help C104`. All charts codes and their meaning: [charts](charts.md); every category: [index](README.md).
