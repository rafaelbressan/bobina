# M140: counter passed its stop value

- Severity: warning (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: markup and data

## What to do

The counter passed its stop: bobina seq set NAME N, a higher stop, or wrap: true.

In the terminal: `bobina help M140`. All markup and data codes and their meaning: [markup and data](markup-and-data.md); every category: [index](README.md).
