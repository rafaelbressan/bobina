# C107: repeated chart labels combined into one point (summed; aggregate= says how)

- Severity: note (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: charts

## What to do

Nothing to fix when that is what you meant (a row per product, summed per day). aggregate=max or aggregate=mean combines the rows of a label otherwise.

In the terminal: `bobina help C107`. All charts codes and their meaning: [charts](charts.md); every category: [index](README.md).
