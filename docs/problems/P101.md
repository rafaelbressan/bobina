# P101: the paper roll is almost over (estimate)

- Severity: warning (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: printer and paper

## What to do

Have a new roll ready; after loading it: bobina paper new LENGTH.

In the terminal: `bobina help P101`. All printer and paper codes and their meaning: [printer and paper](printer-and-paper.md); every category: [index](README.md).
