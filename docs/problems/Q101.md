# Q101: QR module too small to scan reliably

- Severity: warning (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: codes

## What to do

Use a bigger module: ```qr {module=4}``` (3 is the native minimum, 4 in raster).

Evidence: printed test N6, R5-05 (docs/printers/mtp-ii-58.md).

In the terminal: `bobina help Q101`. All codes: [index](README.md).
