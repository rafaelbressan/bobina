# L110: a column that did not fit moved to a new row

- Severity: note (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: layout

## What to do

Nothing to fix if that is what you want. To keep the columns on one row, give them smaller width= or use wrap=nowrap: their content squeezes (text wraps, QR codes and images shrink).

In the terminal: `bobina help L110`. All codes: [index](README.md).
