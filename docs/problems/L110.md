# L110: a column that did not fit moved to a new row

- Severity: note (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: layout

## What to do

Nothing to fix if that is what you want. With wrap=wrap: to keep the columns on one row, give them smaller width= or use wrap=nowrap (their content squeezes: text wraps, QR codes and images shrink). More columns than the paper has letters for (one each, and the gaps): use fewer columns, or gap=0.

In the terminal: `bobina help L110`. All layout codes and their meaning: [layout](layout.md); every category: [index](README.md).
