# B101: stroke thinner than 2 dots prints broken

- Severity: warning (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: drawing

## What to do

Draw lines at least 2 dots thick (thickness=2), or keep it and silence it with --allow B101.

Evidence: printed test R4-01 (docs/printers/mtp-ii-58.md).

In the terminal: `bobina help B101`. All codes: [index](README.md).
