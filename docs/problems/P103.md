# P103: the roll is not as wide as this printer's paper (recorded anyway)

- Severity: warning (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: printer and paper

## What to do

This roll is for another printer: load one as wide as the profile's paper (57 and 58 mm are the same rolls), or record it for the printer it fits (--printer).

In the terminal: `bobina help P103`. All codes: [index](README.md).
