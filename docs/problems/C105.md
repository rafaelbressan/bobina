# C105: a chart too small for its labels: fewer or smaller labels

- Severity: note (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: charts

## What to do

Give the chart a taller height= (height=30mm), fewer values (y-ticks=2, x-labels=ends) or a smaller size= for its labels (size=16px).

In the terminal: `bobina help C105`. All charts codes and their meaning: [charts](charts.md); every category: [index](README.md).
