# M122: image removed (not the author's value)

- Severity: warning (never stops the job, except --strict)
- Category: markup and data

## What to do

Images cannot come from a value that is not the author's own (data, or computed in the template): put the image in the document or in its front matter.

In the terminal: `bobina help M122`. All codes: [index](README.md).
